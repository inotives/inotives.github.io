The Technical Elephant in the Room

The stocks with the best fundamentals (CEG, VST — generators directly exposed to PJM pricing) have the worst charts. The stocks with the strongest charts (PWR, GEV — infrastructure/equipment) have already priced in the buildout. The market is rewarding the "shovels" and discounting the "mine owners." This divergence is normal for the early stages of a capex cycle and may resolve as PJM auction results and actual PPA volumes come through.

Verdict

The macro thesis is strong — AI power demand is real, quantified, and already flowing into utility capex plans and PPA contracts. The technical setup across the cohort is deeply bifurcated, creating both opportunity and trap risk.
