The Real Absorption Mechanism

Passive/index funds will be forced buyers once these names join major indices. Recent index rule changes mean inclusion will happen faster than historical precedent. To buy these new stocks, passive funds must sell their existing holdings proportionally — mostly the same large-cap tech (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN, META). Result: mechanical selling pressure on the Mag 7.

"The implied equity supply in a few months creates a math problem. Most of it will come from existing holdings." — Paul Kedrosky

Sector Impact

  • Hyperscaler cloud (AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL): Directly impacted — passive funds sell to fund index inclusion. Anthropic/OpenAI going public removes private AI bet exclusivity.
  • Semiconductors (NVDA, AMD, MU): Less direct. IPOs need NVIDIA GPUs so demand strengthens. But passive rebalancing affects them too.
  • Other AI startups (Mistral, Cohere, Databricks, Scale AI): May face 12–24 month IPO delays as the big three absorb all attention and capital.

7 Retail Allocation — Unprecedented

SpaceX's 30% retail allocation (3× the typical 10%) creates a unique dynamic:

  • More retail participation than any previous mega-IPO
  • Higher retail volatility post-listing — retail tends to flip shares faster
  • Potential for meme-stock-style price action in early days
  • Higher risk of post-listing selling pressure when the expected "pop" doesn't materialize
