📉 Momentum Headwinds

  • Insider lock-up overhang: 95.7% of shares locked. Market knows massive supply arrives ~Dec 2026 (and staggered earlier).
  • Musk governance discount: 85.1% voting control deters ESG and European institutions. Danish pension fund already excluded.
  • $165B IPO wave: SpaceX + OpenAI + Anthropic = $165B in new issuance. Liquidity absorption is a macro headwind for all three.

Risk Factors

Events that could break the bull case and drive SPCX toward fair-value levels.

High

Float Overwhelmed by Retail

4.3% float with 30% retail allocation creates a dangerous dynamic. If retail demand is as massive as expected, Nasdaq opening cross could gap SPCX to $250-300, creating a "gap and crap" pattern where anyone who bought above $200 loses 50% in the consolidation. Low float + high retail = violent moves both ways.

High

Q2 Earnings Reality Check

First public earnings (~Nov 2026) will be the first time SPCX faces GAAP scrutiny. If xAI losses accelerate, the 93x revenue multiple compresses violently. Path to $60-80/share if Starlink growth slows below 30% and xAI burn exceeds $3B/qtr.

Medium

Index Inclusion Timeline

Nasdaq 100 eligibility requires 3 months of trading. S&P 500 requires GAAP profitability (negative for now). Passive buyers won't arrive immediately. If the IPO pop is driven entirely by retail speculation, the stock could drift for months before institutional buying stabilizes it.

Medium

Musk Liquidity Event

Musk owns ~42% economic stake (~$735B at $135). History shows he sells when he needs cash (TSLA 2021-22). SPCX lock-up expires staggered from ~Sep 2026, with Musk's 366-day lock-up ending June 2027. Any Form 4 filing from Musk after lock-up expiry would crater the stock.
