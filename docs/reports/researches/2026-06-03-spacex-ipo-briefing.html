PRICE CORRECTION — June 3 Breaking News
Yesterday's report estimated $525-600/share. On June 3, SpaceX fixed the price at $135/share — a take-it-or-leave-it price, no range, no book-building. The $1.75T valuation is unchanged. The difference: the $525-600 estimate assumed ~3.32B fully-diluted shares (pre-xAI-merger). After the xAI merger, total shares outstanding is ~12.96B ($1.75T / $135). The 555.6M new shares at $135 raise the $75B as planned. This is the largest per-share price discrepancy between analyst estimates and actual pricing in IPO history, entirely driven by merger-related share dilution.

Key Risks

The six most material risk factors that could move SPCX post-IPO.

High

xAI Losses & Accumulated Deficit

xAI burns $2.5B/quarter. Accumulated deficit of $41.3B. Starlink profit ($1.19B/qtr) covers less than half of AI losses. Path to consolidated profitability is unclear.

High

Scarcity Premium vs Fundamentals

SOTP fair value derived by Aardvark Labs: $1.35-1.55T. IPO target of $1.75T sits 10-30% above. The "scarcity premium" may not survive past lock-up expiry when shares become available.

Medium

Musk Voting Control (85.1%)

Dual-class structure gives Musk 85.1% voting power with only ~42% economic stake. May deter European institutions. Danish pension fund AkademikerPension already excluded on governance grounds.

Medium

Tight Float / Volatility Risk

Only ~4.3% of equity floats at IPO (~$75B of $1.75T). With 30% retail allocation and massive anticipated demand, first-day volatility could be extreme. Low float + high retail = squeeze risk both ways.

Medium

2026 Mega-IPO Wave Liquidity Absorption

SpaceX ($75B) + OpenAI (~$50B) + Anthropic (~$40B) = ~$165B in new issuance. Combined market cap impact of ~$3.7T. Historical precedent: Aramco 2019 depressed EM flows for a quarter.

Low

Starship Operational Risk

SpaceX is three businesses (Starlink, Launch, xAI) but the $1.75T valuation assumes Starship success. May 22 test deployed mock satellites but booster failed to land. Starship delays directly impact the valuation thesis.
